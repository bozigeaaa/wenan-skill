# Title Selection

## Title Job

The title must do three things:

1. Hook with the hotspot.
2. Show the Saudi engineering/temporary construction relationship.
3. Avoid exaggerating the consequence.

## Candidate Generation

Generate three title candidates internally:

- Curiosity title: asks what the hotspot changes.
- Practical title: names the project-side thing to recheck.
- Risk title: names the hidden risk without panic.

Choose the one with the strongest truth-to-click ratio.

## 本地交付能力触发条件

When the verified hotspot directly concerns shipping, ports, sailing schedules, imports, 跨境采购, arrival-to-site delivery, warehousing, distribution, installation, or delivery responsibility, generate one additional internal candidate from the **local delivery capability** perspective.

- Ask whether the Saudi-local production, regular-stock allocation, parts supply, delivery, installation, and follow-up responsibility chain can actually support the project.
- Prefer titles that teach the buyer how to verify real local delivery capability; do not turn the title into a generic claim that all local suppliers are better.
- Do not default to China-side or overseas-shipment framing. Mention China, overseas sources, imports, or 跨境采购 only when that pathway is a verified and necessary part of the specific topic.
- For fire, approval, material, technical-selection, or camp-operation topics, do not force a local-delivery title unless local fulfilment responsibility is the verified core question.

Examples for delivery-related hotspots:

- “红海风险上升后，沙特项目方该怎么判断本地供应商有没有真交付能力？”
- “临建交付别只看船期：本地生产、配送和安装谁来接？”

## Good Title Patterns

- "霍尔木兹一紧张，沙特工地最先该重算什么？"
- "中东航运一波动，沙特项目交付为什么会跟着紧张？"
- "国际油价一动，沙特营地预算最容易漏算哪一项？"
- "一个国际热点，为什么会影响沙特工地的采购节奏？"

## Avoid

- "霍尔木兹停关，沙特项目全线停摆"
- "中东变天，临建行业要爆了"
- "所有货船都绕行，材料马上暴涨"
- "这次事件让东方骆驼机会来了"

## Final Title Check

Before finalizing, ask internally:

- Does the title rely on a verified fact?
- Does it name one industry relationship?
- Would the title still be true if the situation improves tomorrow?
- Is it professional enough for B2B viewers?
- If it uses the local-delivery angle, does the verified topic actually concern delivery or cross-border dependence rather than an unrelated technical issue?
