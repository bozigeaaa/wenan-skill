# Saudi Breakout Story Script

## Purpose

Create Chinese short-video scripts that help 东方骆驼 accounts break out of a narrow product audience by telling Saudi local life, hot topic, counterintuitive, or industry-observation stories. The default goal is attention and trust, not selling.

## Core Rule

Do not mention 东方骆驼, TBOX, light steel, fire systems, or any company/product unless the user explicitly asks. A breakout story should make viewers think "this person really understands Saudi", not "this is an ad".

## Required Sources

Before writing:

- Read `knowledge/AI资料导航索引.md` when local Saudi industry facts may matter.
- Read `knowledge/沙特临建行业认知.xlsx` for Saudi construction, temporary camp, approval, government, supply-chain, or engineering context.
- Browse current reliable sources when the topic involves today's news, current policy, trending events, public figures, sports, visas, labor rules, prices, market changes, or anything likely to change.

## Workflow

1. Identify the audience curiosity.
   - What would make a non-customer stop scrolling?
   - Choose one surprise, conflict, or misunderstood local rule.

2. Verify the factual spine.
   - Read `references/fact-boundaries.md`.
   - Do not invent personal encounters, local rumors, fines, crackdowns, or "everyone knows" claims.

3. Build the story.
   - Read `references/story-pattern.md`.
   - Use one core point: surprising local behavior, hidden business logic, real industry boundary, or contrast between Chinese intuition and Saudi reality.

4. Keep it product-free.
   - If the topic naturally touches temporary camps or construction, explain the industry logic without turning it into a product pitch.

5. Output only the complete script by default.

## Tone

Curious, vivid, grounded, and human. It can be sharper and more entertaining than product scripts, but must not mock Saudi people, local religion, local suppliers, workers, or Chinese contractors.

## Reference Files

- `references/story-pattern.md`: breakout story structure and opening patterns.
- `references/fact-boundaries.md`: current-news, anecdote, and culture-safety boundaries.
