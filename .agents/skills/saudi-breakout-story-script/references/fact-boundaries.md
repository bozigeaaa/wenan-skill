# Fact Boundaries

## Must Verify

Browse current sources for:

- News and public events.
- Visa, labor, tax, customs, government, or policy changes.
- Current prices, exchange rates, fines, or enforcement actions.
- Public project timelines and public figures.
- Competitor or market trend claims.

Use local knowledge files for:

- Saudi temporary construction/camp industry cognition.
- Company-provided project/product facts when the user explicitly asks to connect the story to business.

## Do Not Invent

- Personal encounters: "我一个客户就遇到过..."
- Extreme scams, raids, fines, or shutdowns.
- Worker protests or collective action.
- Religious/cultural claims without context.
- "Saudi people all..." generalizations.

## Safe Framing

- "在一些大型项目里..."
- "如果是政府、PIF、Aramco 这类体系，逻辑会更复杂。"
- "这不是所有项目都一样，关键要看业主、地区和合同要求。"
- "我更建议把它理解成一种项目管理逻辑，而不是简单的当地习惯。"
