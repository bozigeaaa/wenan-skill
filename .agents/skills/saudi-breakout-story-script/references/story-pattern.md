# Breakout Story Pattern

## Good Topic Shapes

- Saudi local rule that Chinese viewers easily misunderstand.
- A construction-site or camp-management detail that reveals a larger Saudi business logic.
- A current public topic explained from the perspective of "what it means for people working in Saudi".
- A contrast: "In China you may think X, but in Saudi projects the real issue is Y."

## Default Structure

1. Hook with a surprising but fair observation.
2. Explain the common misunderstanding.
3. Give the real local or industry reason.
4. Add one grounded scene or consequence.
5. End with a question, next-topic teaser, or self-check.

## Opening Patterns

- "很多第一次来沙特的人，都会误会一件事。"
- "这件事在国内看起来很奇怪，但在沙特项目上非常正常。"
- "别只看热闹，这个新闻背后其实藏着一个沙特项目逻辑。"
- "如果你准备来沙特做工程，有个细节最好提前知道。"

## Endings

- "你还想听哪个沙特项目里的反常识？评论区丢给我。"
- "下期我继续讲一个很多人来沙特以后才明白的规则。"
- "这类事看着小，其实背后都是当地商业和工程逻辑。"

## Avoid

- Turning the story into a product pitch.
- Stereotyping Saudi people, workers, religious customs, weather, or local suppliers.
- Claiming "所有沙特项目都这样" when it only applies to a specific region, project type, owner, or contractor.
